# 0044: The order after G1's area A: finish G1, the gems in between

The owner asked (2026-11-27), with G1's start area and area A installed
(decisions/0040, 0043): "Should we finish overhauling all the props and
vegetation in the first level first, or maybe remaster the gems a bit?"

What weighed:
- **G1 as the whole recipe.** Each level's terrain goes in its own
  overhaul (models-plan M6). Finishing G1, with its ground, buildings and
  plants, shows the full overhaul of one level before it is repeated.
- **G1's buildings are scenery with baked light,** like the ground: the
  walls, buildings, barn, fences, planks, platforms and doors that the
  rest of G1's brief left out (decisions/0041). The light carry-over and
  the checkers work on them as they are.
- **Plants need the game first** (models-plan M7):
  - The model pack takes opaque materials only; every brief so far has
    asked for opaque PNGs. Plants need cut-out or see-through leaves.
  - The plant effects of decisions/0017 (sway, edges, light through)
    need to reach replaced plants.
- **The gems** (the crystals, runestones and gargoyle items of
  decisions/0018) are pickups in every level, as the treasure pickups
  were (decisions/0031). One small brief improves every level.

Decision (the owner's "Yes, that order"):
1. G1's ground areas B, C and D (decisions/0041), under way.
2. The junk chest's props (art-briefs/junk-chest-props.md, written
   2026-11-26 and still current; resent to the owner 2026-11-27).
3. The gems for the art tool, while the game gets plant support (cut-out
   materials in the model pack, the plant effects on replaced plants) and
   G1's load time is looked at. G1 loads 2.0 s longer with area A,
   decisions/0043.
4. G1's buildings and plants, which finish the level.

The art tool works one job at a time, so the numbers order its work. The
game's own work in step 3 does not wait on the art.
